The ritual

Buransh is a small tea house where Rajpur Road starts to climb. We brew slowly, cook what the hills grow, and never rush you off a window seat.

Named after the red rhododendron that covers Uttarakhand every spring. Come for a cup, stay for the fog lifting off the valley.
